How'd We Meet?

We met each other in a COMP 2511 class, that is, Namrata's Web Development class. We found each other to be pretty chill and interesting, so we decided to talk a bit more after class, grabbing some lunch at Wyckham. Since then, we've been just getting to know each other and decided why not collaborate to make a project?

Why'd We Make This?

We decided to make this website due to two main reasons, one being that it was a required assignment for COMP 2511, and also because we thought it would be cool to have a place to talk about and share some of our interests. We decided to collaborate in making a website where people could get to know us and also just to have a place to update whats going on with our lives.
