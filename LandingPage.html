Welcome

We are Lorenzo and Jalen! Climbers, hikers, and adventurers based in Calgary. This site is where we share our hobbies and updates.

Skip to Main Content

Welcome to our shared corner of the internet. This site was built as part of our COMP 2511 Web Development course at Mount Royal University, but it also doubles as a real space where we get to share what we are up to. Click through to our individual pages to learn more about each of us.

Lorenzo is a Computer Information Systems student at MRU with a passion for climbing, hiking, drawing, and sewing. You can usually find him on the MRU climbing wall working on a new route or out on a trail somewhere in the Rockies. His blog has more details on his hobbies and what he has been up to lately.

Jalen is also a Computer Information Systems student at MRU originally from Winnipeg, Manitoba. He is a DJ and aspiring music producer who is always working on something new. In May 2025 he will be making the move out to British Columbia to continue his education and push his art career forward. Check out his blog for more.

We met in Namrata's COMP 2511 class and decided to collaborate on this project together. Outside of school we both share a love of the outdoors and being active. This site is a work in progress and we plan to keep updating it as things happen. Thanks for stopping by and feel free to explore the pages above.
